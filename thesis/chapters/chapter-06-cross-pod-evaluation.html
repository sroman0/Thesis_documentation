Chapter 6 Cross-Pod Evidence Brief

Current baseline: Chapter 6 uses only the completed cross-Pod Minikube experiment. The older one-Pod laboratory and its five-replication benchmark are excluded.

Evaluation Argument

The chapter asks whether a privileged monitoring Pod can observe and correlate the AES-beta exploit in an independent target Pod when the client runs in a third Pod on the same node. It then measures monitor user-space CPU and memory with and without exact kernel UID filtering.

Permitted Results

  • 3/3 benign controls completed with zero AES-beta alerts.
  • 3/3 exploits recovered the flag with 1,793 workers and one complete alert each.
  • Filtered CPU: 0.46% mean of one core, SD 0.02, range 0.44–0.47%, mean run P95 1.00%.
  • Unfiltered CPU: 5.73% mean; filtered reduction 91.97%.
  • Filtered maxima: 92.17 MiB cgroup memory and 36.97 MiB RSS.
  • No diagnosed loss or restart delta in the six monitored runs.
Forbidden inference: do not call 0.46% total overhead; do not use no-monitor duration to infer slowdown; do not generalise three scenario runs into detection accuracy or production capacity.
