Editorial Decision

Chapter 6 is scenario-led. Its central experiment is the AES-beta CTF workload executed in a Minikube Pod containing target, trigger and monitor containers. The narrative follows controlled input, observed kernel evidence, collective alert and resource measurements.

The older outline has been corrected. There is no standalone point- detector case study because the implemented experiment directly evaluates one collective detector. There is no separate ATT&CK coverage section: the two ATT&CK mappings explain alert interpretation but cannot establish technique coverage. Functional validation and performance evaluation remain separate because their evidence has different maturity.

Scoped performance claim. Five replicated runs per profile are now available. Agents may verify the filtered user-space monitor result below 5%, but must not convert it into a total-overhead claim because kernel attribution remains incomplete.
