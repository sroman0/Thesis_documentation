Independent Technical and Editorial Review of Chapter 4

Decision: conditionally acceptable after technical-baseline repair and editorial compression. The hook-focused structure is sound, all required host hook families are represented, and the chapter normally preserves the distinction between logical events, physical probes and observation stages. It should not yet be frozen because the recorded committed baseline is not buildable and does not satisfy its own probe-registry test.

The highest-priority textual correction is in Section 4.2: the common initialiser does substantially more than establish identity and timing data. The largest editorial issue is repetition of concepts already owned by Chapters 2 and 3, especially in Sections 4.4--4.7.
