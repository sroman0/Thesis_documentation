Distinct Pod UIDs, IPs, container IDs and /proc/1/cgroup paths were recorded. Each cgroup path contains its corresponding Pod UID, establishing separate pod cgroups on the shared node.

Security Contexts and Mounts

  • All effective Pod specifications preserve hostPID: false, hostNetwork: false and shareProcessNamespace: false; a false Boolean may be omitted from API-rendered YAML and is also preserved in the last-applied configuration.
  • Target and trigger run as UID/GID 10001 and 10002 respectively, drop all capabilities and use read-only root filesystems.
  • The monitor alone is privileged and mounts host BTF, bpffs and tracefs.
  • The Secret volume appears only in the target Deployment.
  • Automatic ServiceAccount token mounting is disabled.

Monitor Startup

The monitor opened its embedded eBPF object with host BTF, loaded one policy from the image and one detector from the mounted ConfigMap, selected open, sched_process_exec and security_file_open, and enabled exact kernel UID filtering for UID 10001. It remained in alerts-only mode. The image-backed policy configuration was revalidated on 19 August 2026 after replacing a stale Minikube image; the resulting monitor Pod was Ready with zero restarts.

The target listener reported readiness on [::]:1337 as UID/GID 10001. Listener startup alone did not establish reachability; the later Phase 5 connection through the ClusterIP Service did.
